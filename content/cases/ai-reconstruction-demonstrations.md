## External demonstrations

Bilawal Sidhu's accounts connect God's Eye View with WorldFX demonstrations and Astra-assisted work on editable 3D scenes. They illustrate the author's experiments with spatial presentation and reconstruction. They do not establish that this local hub can reconstruct an arbitrary video, room or historical event.

The two primary accounts are already covered by the Library's reviewed original summaries: [the release account](#/library/knowledge/i-open-sourced-gods-eye-view-summary) and [the later WorldFX/Astra account](#/library/knowledge/gods-eye-view-blew-up-then-astra-summary). Those summaries link to the originals and distinguish the author's claims from capabilities verified in the pinned checkout. This case adds no new claim of independent reproduction.

## What is local

The local globe has documented layers, authored scenes and specific bounded reconstruction tools. Its recent-launch paths use estimates; its Nepal scene uses licensed bundled material. These concrete features should be evaluated through their own sources and limits. The [pinned project overview](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/README.md) does not make every external demonstration part of the local code.

WorldFX/Astra demonstrations remain external proprietary work. This page is **demonstration-only** and offers no local reconstruction action. It does not call a model or provider, configure a credential, retrieve video or request a paid service. The optional AI features elsewhere in the globe do not change this boundary.
