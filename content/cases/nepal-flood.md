## What the bundled scene shows

The Nepal case places licensed event material around the Bhote Koshi river corridor. The documented flood occurred on August 26, 2026. The bundled comparison uses an October 16, 2021 Vantor image beside an August 27, 2026 image. These are **Static reference assets**, not a current feed. The older image provides historical context; it is not an immediate pre-event baseline.

The [pinned event provenance](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/public/events/bhote-koshi-2026/README.md) records the imagery, GeoPera-derived centerline, source modifications and interpretation limits. Clouds remain in the post-event image. Sensor, season and development differences also matter when comparing the two dates.

## Open the geographic view

The globe action opens the river area on Esri imagery and enables the two bundled Bhote Koshi layers. It does not select a Director scene, shot, evidence card or playback position. Choose the bundled Nepal/Bhote Koshi scene in Director to explore its authored sequence. The [scene recipe](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/src/scenes/recipes.js) and [map fallback policy](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/src/scenes/packs/nepal.js) describe the existing interaction; this page adds no replay data.

## Read the corridor carefully

The animated corridor is a schematic progression along a published centerline. Its width and timing do not model inundation extent or flood arrival. It cannot support emergency planning, exposure estimates or casualty claims. Linked witness material has separate provenance; capture times are not established simply by placement in the scene. Some evidence locations lie outside the comparison rectangle.
