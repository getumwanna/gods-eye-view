## Demonstration, not an incident record

The globe can show available aircraft contacts and paths. That capability does not supply an imported historical accident track, a complete aircraft population or a finding about an incident's cause. This case is a **demonstration-only** account of how to separate those evidence types. It has no incident reconstruction to launch.

The [pinned flight layer](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/src/layers/flights/index.js) and [data-source documentation](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/DATA_SOURCES.md) describe the current local tools. A provider contact is an observation subject to that source's coverage and timing. A modeled or interpolated position is an estimate. Aircraft metadata can assist interpretation but is not an independent confirmation of an accident narrative.

## Ask what supports each claim

A reconstruction would need a defined incident, source records for the relevant time, provenance for each position, and an account of gaps or conflicting evidence. A current view near an airport cannot substitute for those records. An animation does not establish that an aircraft followed the depicted path, and the absence of a contact does not establish that an aircraft was absent.

The project's tracking regression uses synthetic fixtures to check software behavior. Those tests are useful engineering evidence, never observations of a real flight or accident. Keep that distinction when describing a passing harness result.

## Boundaries for local use

No historical incident dataset, recorder or automated archive is introduced here. OpenSky's terms also apply to anonymous access; any later recording proposal needs a terms review before it is proposed. This workspace does not support named-person tracking or identifying people from aircraft activity. Preserve source credits and the [responsible-use guidance](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/SECURITY.md#responsible-use). No globe action is offered because it would suggest an incident state that the local Library cannot restore.
