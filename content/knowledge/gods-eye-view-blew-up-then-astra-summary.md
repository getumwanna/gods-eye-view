# September update: GEV, WorldFX and Astra

## Original publication

- **Title:** [God’s Eye View Blew Up. Astra Showed Up.](https://www.spatialintelligence.ai/p/gods-eye-view-blew-up-then-astra)
- **Author:** Bilawal Sidhu
- **Published:** September 19, 2026
- **This original summary reviewed:** October 21, 2026

## Summary of the author’s account

Sidhu reports community interest following GEV’s open-source release and describes extending its globe with geographically placed material. His Nepal example brings together disaster imagery and material recorded on the ground. Separately, he presents a WorldFX hurricane replay and a reconstruction of a Miami cargo-aircraft accident using several kinds of public evidence.

The article then discusses Astra-assisted work in Blender: turning scans into editable scenes, including an experiment based on a room in his parents’ home. Sidhu describes imperfections in that reconstruction and identifies further refinement as future work. These are the author’s accounts of demonstrations, not independently verified observations or findings about an accident’s cause.

## What the local project supports

This comparison concerns the reviewed upstream revision `064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1`, rather than a hosted service or every experiment in the article.

- **Nepal:** the bundled scene has documented imagery and a schematic flood progression. Its 2021 image provides historical context beside 2026 post-event imagery; it is not an immediate pre-event baseline or a flood-arrival model. See the pinned [event provenance](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/public/events/bhote-koshi-2026/README.md).
- **Hurricanes:** local NHC/CPHC advisories cover the Atlantic and eastern/central North Pacific. Weather history is bounded. That partial capability does not reproduce the article’s WorldFX archival replay. See the pinned [source and coverage documentation](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/064d51f7be2675f1c69e16068e9c49d1a60144d1/DATA_SOURCES.md).
- **Aircraft incidents:** current aircraft contacts and flight paths do not supply the Miami investigation’s evidence package or establish a cause. This Library imports no incident dataset.
- **Astra and WorldFX:** the proprietary demonstrations are external to this local project. Reading this note does not enable their reconstruction workflows, obtain their data, or make a model call.
