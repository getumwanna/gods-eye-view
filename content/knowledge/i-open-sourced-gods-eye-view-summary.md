## Summary of the author’s account

Sidhu announces the release of GEV’s source code and invites people to explore and extend it. He describes a globe that brings aircraft, vessel, satellite and camera information together, with visual effects and optional voice interaction. He recommends the repository’s setup guidance and explains his effort to make a useful starting experience possible with limited reliance on paid APIs.

The post also distinguishes that open foundation from the expense of assembling and serving detailed historical data, pointing toward separate future work. It is a dated release announcement and the author’s description of his project, rather than independent confirmation that every feed is available in every place or session.

Actual coverage, credentials, source timestamps and feed health still matter. The broad announcement is not a reason to label every displayed value Live. Orbital propagation is an estimate; launch replay uses reconstructed timing and geometry rather than continuous ascent telemetry. Weather history and available imagery also have product, date and regional limits. Historical hurricane or incident demonstrations should not be assumed to be available as local archives.

This customization keeps persistent secrets on the server and leaves optional provider access to the user’s POWER UP setup. Its Data Sources directory describes reference facts; it does not acquire feeds or report their current health. Hosting remains undecided, and this Library adds neither a recorder nor historical data collection.
